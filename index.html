Process parameters

Model assumptions
  • Finite circular source and mask-substrate gap produce a geometric penumbra.
  • Atoms are emitted with a cosine-weighted angular distribution from the source aperture.
  • Surface diffusion is modeled as a Gaussian convolution with diffusion length √(4Dτ).
  • Diffusion coefficients use an Arrhenius law with editable material presets.

How fitted outputs are calculated

FWHM linewidth
Distance between the left and right centerline-profile crossings at 50% of peak normalized thickness.
10–90% edge width
Distance along the leading edge between the 10% and 90% normalized-thickness crossings; smaller values indicate sharper edges.
Geometric blur σ
Estimated standard deviation of the finite-source penumbra: mask-substrate gap × source diameter ÷ source-mask gap, converted from full blur to σ by dividing by 2.355.
Diffusion blur σ
Half of the Arrhenius diffusion length √(4Dτ), where D = D₀ exp(−Eₐ/kBT) and τ is scaled by deposition time and sticking.
Total edge σ
Quadrature combination of geometric and diffusion blur, with a small source-temperature flux-energy correction.
Al diffusion coefficient
Arrhenius diffusion coefficient for the selected substrate preset at the current substrate temperature.

Calibration note: use experimental profiles to tune activation energy, prefactor, residence time, sticking, and source angular distribution. The simulator is intended for corroboration and sensitivity analysis, not as a substitute for direct metrology.
